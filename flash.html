Plug it in
Connect the Atom Lite to this computer with a USB-C cable. Use Chrome or Edge — Safari and Firefox can't do this. No drivers needed.
2
Flash it
Click the button below, pick the device's serial port when your browser asks, and wait about a minute.
If the button doesn't do anything, you're not on Chrome or Edge.

Done
